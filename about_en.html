Your daily companion for memorizing & reviewing the Holy Quran

Our Mission

El Moudhakir (inspired by verse 17 of Surah Al-Qamar) was designed to offer students, memorizers (Huffadh), and Quran readers a modern, precise tool that respects the sacred text.

﴿ وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا الْقُرْآنَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ ﴾
Surah Al-Qamar • Verse 17

The application offers the two major authentic readings: Hafs 'an 'Asim (Medina Mushaf) and Warsh 'an Nafi' (Maghrebi Mushaf), with verse-by-verse audio and dynamic text tracking.

Key Features & Gesture Shortcuts

  • Personalized reminders based on your mastery of each page: The optimal reminder dates are calculated using the SM-2 (SuperMemo-2) spaced repetition algorithm.
  • Verse / Page / Surah Recitation: High-quality audio recitation with reciter selection and highlighted tracking.
  • Custom repetitions: Repetition loops per verse or per page (or both combined) to strengthen auditory memory.
  • Hidden Recitation Mode (Hifdh): Tap the eye icon at the top to hide the verses and recite from memory before revealing.
👆 Double-tap on the study screen:
Instantly enlarges or shrinks the size of the Arabic calligraphy according to your visual comfort.
🔁 Long press on the Loop button:
Opens the selection window to precisely choose the number of repetitions (3×, 5×, 7×, 10×...).
📑 Long press on a Surah or Page:
Directly changes its status to “Mastered” (> 90 days) or “Not started”.

Privacy & Data

Your spiritual progress belongs to you. El Moudhakir does not sell any personal data. Your review statistics and settings are stored securely and privately.
