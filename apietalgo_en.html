Algorithms & APIs Used

Documentation of the data sources and of the teaching algorithm used for memorization

SM-2 Memorization Algorithm

To optimize the time spent learning the Holy Quran and to prevent forgetting, the application integrates the SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) algorithm, based on Hermann Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve.

Each page has its own Ease Factor (EF) and Review Interval (I), automatically recalculated after each session according to your self-assessment and the cumulative number of reviews for that page:

🔴 Review again (Interval reset to 1 day)
🟠 Hard (Short interval)
🟢 Good (Regular interval)
⭐ Perfect (Long interval)

The dates of the review notifications you receive depend on the intervals calculated for each page.

APIs & Text and Audio Data

The authenticity and fidelity of the sacred texts rely on open data banks that are verified and recognized by the community:

UmmahAPI HAFS • TRANSLATION • TRANSLIT.
Provides the Arabic text according to the Hafs reading (Uthmani Rasm), phonetic transliterations in Latin characters, and translations in several languages.
https://ummahapi.com/api/quran/
Fawaz Ahmed Quran API WARSH BY PAGE (604)
CDN distribution of the 604 pages of the Mushaf according to the Riwayah of Warsh 'an Nafi' (specific to the Maghreb and West Africa).
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/quran-api@1/editions/ara-quranwarsh/pages/$page.json
EveryAyah VERSE-BY-VERSE AUDIO
Audio platform providing synchronized verse-by-verse recitations in MP3 format (Sheikh Yassin Al-Jazaery, Sheikh Ibrahim Al-Dosari, etc.).
https://everyayah.com

Respectful Call Interruption

The application uses Android system hardware streams (PhoneState and RealVolume) to silence the phone's ringtone as soon as an incoming call is detected, so that the verse being played can finish without an abrupt cut of the divine word.
At the end of the verse, the audio is paused and the phone's ringtone is restored.
