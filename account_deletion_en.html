Account Deletion — El_moudhakir

Developer: IT-Developers

1. Delete directly from the app (Immediate)

  1. Open the El_moudhakir app.
  2. Go to the Settings menu.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

2. Request deletion without the app (Web / Email)

If you have uninstalled the app, send an email from the address associated with your account to: elmoudhakir.support@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”. Your request will be processed within 7 days at most.

3. Data deleted and retained

Deletion permanently and completely erases your authentication account (name, email address) as well as all your memorization data and preferences stored on Firebase Firestore. No personal data is retained after deletion.
